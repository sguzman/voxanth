THE METHOD / HOW IT WORKS

Voice, from
first principles.

A voice can be explored as a sound source interacting with resonant cavities. Voxanth starts with a compact, inspectable source-filter model and grows from there.

01 / SOURCE

Generate the excitation.

Pitch, harmonic structure, breath, and vibrato begin at the simulated sound source. No voice recording is necessary.

02 / RESONANCE

Shape the spectrum.

Formant frequencies emphasize different resonances, creating distinguishable vowels and timbres that can be tuned continuously.

03 / PERCEPTION

Let your ears decide.

Compare voices, save profiles, and iterate toward a result you actually want to listen to.

RESEARCH / THE NEXT HORIZON

From tones
to conversation.

Intelligibility is an engineering target, not a marketing claim. Speech requires consonants, transitions, timing, and prosody—not just pleasing vowels.

01 · ACTIVE

Acoustic sandbox

Vowels, source-filter controls, profile design, local synthesis and WAV export.

02 · NEXT

Real phoneme mapping

Expand the English pronunciation lexicon, consonant synthesis and a reproducible English test corpus.

03 · PLANNED

Dynamic articulation

Better consonants, coarticulation, tract geometry, acoustic tube modeling and transitions.

04 · PLANNED

Measurable speech

Prosody, blind transcription benchmarks, and reliable intelligible voices.
